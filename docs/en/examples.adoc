== Sample messages

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== accident.xml – lorry accident with a road closure

The publication contains one situation: an accident of a lorry on road 450 near the village of Staré Město
(Bruntál district) and the resulting closure of the road in both directions.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/accident.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `sit:SituationPublication` with the `profileName` and `profileVersion` attributes
identifying the format; `feedType` refers to the data source, `publicationTime` is the time the content was
created and `publicationCreator` identifies NDIC.

Situation::
The situation has two records in version 2; `situationVersionTime` equals the time of their last change.
The first record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` describes the impact – a road closure
(`roadClosed`) in both directions (`applicableForTrafficDirection` = `bothWays`) with remaining capacity 0
in both directions (`impact` and `impactOnOppositeDirection`) and the cause `earlierIncident`. The second
record of type `Accident` describes the cause – an accident involving a lorry
(`accidentInvolvingHeavyLorries`, `vehicleType` = `lorry`) with limited visibility as the cause of the
accident; `safetyRelatedMessage` marks it as SRTI information.

Validity::
Both records are valid from 11{nbsp}March 2023 9:30 to 11{nbsp}November 2023 12:35 (`overallStartTime`,
`overallEndTime`).

Location::
The point-like event is described by the linear location `SingleRoadLinearLocation` using OpenLR, ALERT-C
(table 25, version 9.0, locations 11896 → 11895; `alertCAffectedDirection` = `both` for the closure in both
directions, `aligned` for the accident), Global Network section 1620017 and the location path
`gmlLineString`. The closure covers the whole section (0 → 100{nbsp}%), the accident a short section of
about 50{nbsp}m at the accident site (21.01 → 70.08{nbsp}%).

Other content::
`source` gives the originator of the information (Police of the Czech Republic, PČR),
`generalPublicComment` a free-text description and `urlLink` a link to details.

=== restriction.xml – roadworks, alternating one-lane traffic

The publication contains a partial closure of road I/10 in Desná (Jablonec nad Nisou district) due to
construction works with alternating traffic in one lane.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/restriction.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
A record of type `ConstructionWorks` describes the cause (construction works, `constructionWork`), a record
of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` the impact (alternating traffic in one lane,
`singleAlternateLineTraffic`, mandatory – `complianceOption` = `mandatory`). The value
`probabilityOfOccurrence` = `certain` – the closure is already in force at the time of publication. Both
records are in version 6.

Location::
The location is described by all four methods: OpenLR, ALERT-C, chainage of road 10 (106 797–107 507 m)
and Global Network sections of version 22.12 – five sections in travel order (126853–2901326) – and by the
location path `gmlLineString`.

=== closure-junction.xml – closure of a junction with three arms

Full closure of the junction of roads II/259 and III/25924 in Vrátno (Mladá Boleslav district) due to sewer
construction. The sections meet in one node and do not form a contiguous chain, so the location is a group of
sections.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-junction.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 5: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory (`complianceOption` = `mandatory`).

Validity::
From 7{nbsp}October 2026 10:01 to 31{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`LocationGroupByList` with three linear locations, each on one road: road II/259 (two Global Network sections
in travel order), an unnumbered connecting link (one section) and road III/25924 (one section). Each location
carries its own location path `gmlLineString`; the sections serve both directions
(`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).

=== closure-route.xml – closure along two adjoining roads

Full closure of roads III/34041 and III/34042 in Úhřetice (Chrudim district) due to road surface repair. The
location runs contiguously from one road to the other, so it is a route of two parts.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-route.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 4: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory.

Validity::
From 21{nbsp}September 2026 7:00 to 1{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`ItineraryByIndexedLocations` with two parts in travel order: part 1 on road III/34041 (four Global Network
sections), part 2 on the adjoining road III/34042 (four sections). Each part is a linear location
`SingleRoadLinearLocation` with its own location path `gmlLineString`; the end of the first part is the start of
the second. The sections serve both directions (`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).
