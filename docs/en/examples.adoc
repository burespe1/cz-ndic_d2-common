== Message examples

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== accident.xml – accident with an obstruction and a road closure

The publication contains one situation: an accident on road I/20 near Sedlec (České Budějovice district), an
obstruction on the carriageway and intermittent short-term closures of the road.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/accident.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `sit:SituationPublication` with the attributes `@profileName` and `@profileVersion`
identifying the format; `feedType` refers to the distribution (data source), `publicationTime` is the time the publication was
generated and `publicationCreator` identifies NDIC.

Situation::
The situation has three records in version 6 from the Police (`PCR`); `situationVersionTime` equals the time of
their last change. The record of type `sit:Accident` describes the accident (`accident`), the record
`sit:GeneralObstruction` an obstruction on the carriageway (`objectOnTheRoad`) and the record
`sit:RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` the consequence – intermittent short-term closures
(`intermittentShortTermClosures`), mandatory (`complianceOption` = `mandatory`). Cause and consequence are separate
records of one situation; the comment is given in each of them.

Validity::
All records are valid on 1{nbsp}October 2026 from 12:10 to 15:10 (`overallStartTime`, `overallEndTime`).

Location::
The point-like event is described by a short section of road I/20 (about 10{nbsp}m) by ALERT-C (table 25,
version 11.0, locations 46501 → 3559, `alertCAffectedDirection` = `aligned`), OpenLR, chainage of road 20
(192 487–192 497 m), Global Network section 2808505 of version 26.06 (7.26 → 8.69{nbsp}%) and the location path
`gmlLineString`.

=== restriction.xml – roadworks, alternate one-way traffic

Planned roadworks on road I/14 in Dolní Radechová (Náchod district) with alternate one-way traffic in one lane.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/restriction.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
The record of type `sit:MaintenanceWorks` describes the cause (roadworks, `roadworks`, subject of works the road
surface – `road`), the record of type `sit:RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` the impact (alternate one-way traffic,
`singleAlternateLineTraffic`, mandatory – `complianceOption` = `mandatory`). The works are planned, so
`probabilityOfOccurrence` = `certain` and the validity starts in the future. Both records are in version 2.

Validity::
From 5{nbsp}October 2026 0:00 to 18{nbsp}December 2026 23:59. That traffic is controlled on working days
7:00–16:00 is stated in the comment only.

Location::
The location is described by all methods: OpenLR, ALERT-C (locations 26094 → 23386, `alertCDirectionCoded` =
`negative`), chainage of road 14 (115 498–115 821 m), Global Network sections of version 26.06 (four sections in
travel order, 1678138 from 82.16{nbsp}%, 1456372 and 1456373 whole, 1456374 to 0.88{nbsp}%) and the location path
`gmlLineString`.

=== closure-junction.xml – closure of a junction with three arms

Full closure of the junction of roads II/259 and III/25924 in Vrátno (Mladá Boleslav district) due to sewer
construction. The sections meet in one node and do not form a contiguous chain, so the location is a group of
sections.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-junction.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `sit:RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 5: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory (`complianceOption` = `mandatory`).

Validity::
From 7{nbsp}October 2026 10:01 to 31{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`loc:LocationGroupByList` with three linear locations, each on one road: road II/259 (two Global Network sections
in travel order), an unnumbered connecting link (one section) and road III/25924 (one section). Each location
carries its own location path `gmlLineString`; the sections serve both directions
(`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).

=== closure-route.xml – closure along two adjoining roads

Full closure of roads III/34041 and III/34042 in Úhřetice (Chrudim district) due to road surface repair. The
location runs contiguously from one road to the other, so it is a route of two parts.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-route.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `sit:RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 4: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory.

Validity::
From 21{nbsp}September 2026 7:00 to 1{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`loc:ItineraryByIndexedLocations` with two parts in travel order: part 1 on road III/34041 (four Global Network
sections), part 2 on the adjoining road III/34042 (four sections). Each part is a linear location
`loc:SingleRoadLinearLocation` with its own location path `gmlLineString`; the end of the first part is the start of
the second. The sections serve both directions (`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).
